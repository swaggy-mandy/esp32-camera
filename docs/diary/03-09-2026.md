### Flash Board
- Two options
  1) Xenon tube
  2) High Power LED
 - HOWEVER, Xenon Style flashes need extremely high voltages, about 200V to 350V charged up in their capacitors😥
 --> Hence, option 2 is being chosen as safety is the #1 priority (wink wink recruiters look at me making wise engineering decisions)

 - According to the Google AI overview, "A camera flash LED printed circuit board (PCB) requires a high-current DC-DC step-up driver and thermal management to handle short bursts of intense light safely."
 - "Key Design RequirementsLED Driver IC: Uses specialized boost converters (like the ams OSRAM AS1170 or Texas Instruments TPS61310) to supply high peak current safely from a 3.7V Li-Po battery.Thermal Management: Requires good copper planes or aluminum-backed (MCPCB) substrates because flash LEDs dissipate high power rapidly during a strobe.Shutter Synchronization: Connects to a camera sensor's STROBE or SHUTTER pin to synchronize the light pulse and prevent rolling-shutter artifacts or underexposure.Protection Features: Includes flash timeout protection, thermal shutdown, and overvoltage lockouts to prevent burning out the surface-mount LEDs."
 - https://www.youtube.com/watch?reload=9&v=gzYvDB7C9O4
 - Unlike a little indicator LED, a camera flash LED may take something like hundreds of milliamps or even amps during a short pulse.
