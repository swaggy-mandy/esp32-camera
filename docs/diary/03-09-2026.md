 ## Thoughts While Working
- It is looking like my camera will be based on the Seeed Studio Xiao ESP32S3 Sense. Although this feels like "cheating" as the board compines the MCU and the Camera together, I have decided that the "electrical engineering" aspect of this project will be to design the PCB that powers the flash. As a well-seasoned digital camera user (the average university girl who likes to take pictures), the most appealing part of using a digicam over an IPhone is the flash, which gives a uniform front-facing lighting to any faces being captured and increases the likelihood of photo success by 10 times combined with the slightly lower quality resolution. IPhone flash pictures often turn out more scary-looking because the software has an "autoadjust" feature that enhances all the wrong parts and increases the contrast in the worst way.
